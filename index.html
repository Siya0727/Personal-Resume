Research Intern, DST Centre of Excellence for Climate Information

May 2026 – July 2026

IIT Delhi

  • Led India's first pan-India tehsil-level (n = 6,667) spatial autocorrelation analysis of southwest monsoon (JJAS) rainfall trends over 41 years (1982–2022), using three independent gridded datasets (MSWEP, IMDAA, IMD).
  • Applied Mann-Kendall tests with Theil-Sen slopes, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction, Global Moran's I (0.853–0.869, p < 0.001) and LISA to quantify spatial coherence of trends.

  • Processed 42 zip archives of daily IMDAA NetCDF files with xarray, geopandas, rasterio, PySAL/esda and pymannkendall.
